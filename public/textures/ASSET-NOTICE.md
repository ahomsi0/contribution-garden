# Texture assets

The forest-floor and bark texture sets in this directory are adapted from
Poly Haven's **Forest Floor** and **Metasequoia Bark** assets.

- Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_floor
- Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/metasequoia_bark
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal

The bundled images are the 1K JPEG diffuse, OpenGL normal, and roughness maps,
renamed for use by Contribution Garden.
